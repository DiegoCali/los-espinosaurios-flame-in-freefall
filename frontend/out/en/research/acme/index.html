HOW IT WAS CALCULATED / Tad

NORMAL OR INVERSE: WHO FEEDS THE FLAME?

In a normal flame, fuel leaves the burner into oxygen. In an inverse flame, oxygen leaves the burner into ethylene. A frozen Ridge regression maps seven initial conditions to a calculated adiabatic temperature.

Without buoyancy the diffusion flame is not stretched upward; the reactant roles at the burner and in the surroundings set its structure.
