01 / WHAT HAPPENS IN SMOKE01 / SAME-R

PARTICLES CHANGE OVER TIME

In microgravity, particles can cluster as smoke ages. This changes their size and number.

CONCEPTUAL DIAGRAM / NOT MICROSCOPY IMAGES

D_m: diameter of average mass. M₀: number concentration (particles/cm³). D_g and AMD are different measures, not synonyms.

HOW IT WAS MEASURED / SMOKE DETECTION

ONE SMOKE PUSH. TWO DETECTION PRINCIPLES.

A smoke push reaches two detectors. The photoelectric detector registers light scattered by particles; the ionization detector registers a change in ion current. The project labels a response with proxy thresholds.

FRACTION OF PUSHES WITH A RESPONSE · n = 45 labelled pushes

ISS PHOTOELECTRIC Scatter drop ≥ 1 V71.1%
IONIZATION Rise ≥ 0.5 V93.3%

Without flow (possible only in microgravity, where there is no buoyant plume) smoke lingers at the source and coagulates: larger particles, fewer of them. Different particle sizes affect the detection principles differently.

WHY DOES THE RESPONSE CHANGE?

DETECTORS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE

With flow, the ionization detector responded to more pushes than the photoelectric detector. Without flow, the opposite occurred. Different particle sizes affect the detection principles differently.

PROXY ≠ REAL ALARM

ISS: scatter drop ≥ 1 V. Ionization: rise ≥ 0.5 V. Probabilities are model proxy responses: they do not represent detection of any real fire, official alarm algorithms, certification or fire risk. The STS alarm is a separate variable.

07 / SCOPE AND PROVENANCELIMITATIONS / SOURCES

WHAT THE MODEL CANNOT ESTABLISHWHAT WE CAN CONCLUDE

A SINGLE CAMPAIGN

Local evidence covers SAME-R, PSI-101, Expedition 24 (2010). It includes neither SAME-1 / PSI-102 nor independent validation on another mission.

DETECTORS USE PROXIES

Project thresholds represent instrumental responses. They do not equal verified flight alarms or a universal alarm probability.

LIMITED SAMPLES

There are only 1–2 labelled no-flow runs per material and none for silicone. Some scenarios are extrapolated. The two tasks use different validation datasets.
