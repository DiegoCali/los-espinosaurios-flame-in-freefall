WHY DOES THE RESPONSE CHANGE?

DETECTORS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE

With flow, the ionization detector responded to more pushes than the photoelectric detector. Without flow, the reverse occurred. Different particle sizes affect detection principles differently.

PROXY ≠ REAL ALARM

ISS: scatter drop ≥ 1 V. Ionization: rise ≥ 0.5 V. These proportions describe smoke tests, not probabilities of a real fire.
