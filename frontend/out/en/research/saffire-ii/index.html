HOW IT WAS MEASURED / CO₂ RESPONSE

FROM A SENSOR TRACE TO ONE NUMBER.

Each SAFFIRE-II test leaves a CO₂ trace. The response is the median of 1-s CO₂ medians at [150, 180) s minus the median at [−30, 0) s, with electrical ignition as t = 0. Pick a test: the number is recomputed live from its real series.

  1. 01 / IGNITION REFERENCEElectrical ignition defines t = 0.
  2. 02 / BASELINE WINDOWMedian CO₂ between −30 s and 0 s.
  3. 03 / RESPONSE WINDOWMedian CO₂ between 150 s and 180 s.
  4. 04 / DIFFERENCEResponse minus baseline: a signed local change in ppm.

In microgravity, combustion products are not carried upward by a buoyant plume; the forced air flow sets how they reach the sensor.

02 / HOW THE VARIABLE IS DERIVED02 / SAFFIRE-II

FROM THE SENSOR TO THE VALUE WE COMPARE

The CO₂ change is not an absolute concentration: it is the difference between two time windows of the signal.

CALCULATION DIAGRAM / NOT A MEASURED SERIES

Median before ignition

02 RESPONSE

150 to 180 s

Median after the start

Δ CO₂ = response median − reference median

WHY TWO WINDOWS?

They compare the same test signal before and after ignition. The value may be positive or negative, depending on the readings and their variations.

A small negative value does not demonstrate net CO₂ consumption: it may reflect mixing or sensor variation.
