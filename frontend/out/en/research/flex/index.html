A decrease in squared diameter indicates consumption, but segmentation, lighting changes and transient stages cause variations.

The reference is a measured sample at t ≈ 0.3 s in each trajectory. D² is calculated from the recorded diameter. No interpolation or smoothing is used; the normalized value is not an absolute diameter.
