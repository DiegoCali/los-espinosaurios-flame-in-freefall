HOW TO READ THE CURVES

NOT ALL SERIES ARE STRAIGHT LINES

A decrease in squared diameter is a useful sign of consumption, but segmentation, lighting changes and transient stages produce variation.

PROCESSED DATA

Normalization uses the nearest measured diameter to 0.3 s in each series, without interpolation. A normalized value is not an absolute diameter.

THESE THREE SERIES ARE EXAMPLES

They are not the records in the model validation table. A D² slope is a derived quantity, not an additional direct measurement.

Two task-specific scikit-learn Random Forest regressors: 500 trees, max_features=0.4, min_samples_leaf=2, max_depth=None, random_state=42. Median imputation with missing indicators → StandardScaler → Random Forest; targets log1p, outputs expm1. Training: 217 duration / 209 rate; validation: 49 per task. Campaign labels sorted lexicographically select NaT as holdout.

Inputs: pressure (mmHg and Pa), O/N/CO/He mole fractions, initial and extinction diameters (mm and m), nine history features and two fuel flags. The CSV CO label differs from YCO2 in the summary; no reconciliation is applied. History features exist for only three source cases. Zero pressure and reused identifiers require source review.
