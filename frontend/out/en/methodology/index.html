AUTORESEARCH.

RESEARCH THROUGH ITERATION.

An agent proposes changes to a model, runs them under fixed rules and compares the result with previous attempts. It keeps verifiable improvements and documents discarded trials.

RESEARCH THROUGH ITERATION.

ORIGIN / ANDREJ KARPATHY · AUTORESEARCHADAPTATION / LOS ESPINOSAURIOS

AutoResearch is a computational search method; it does not observe the flame or establish a physical hypothesis on its own.

05 / SCIENTIFIC RESPONSIBILITY

A BETTER METRIC ≠ A NEW DISCOVERY.

The method can improve models, but scientific conclusions require adequate measurements, validation and human review.AutoResearch is a computational search method; it does not observe the flame or establish a physical hypothesis on its own.

01

REPEATED SELECTION

Trying many ideas on the same validation set can overfit decisions.

02

LABELS AND MEASUREMENTS

Do not infer soot, extinction or hazard from a surrogate variable.

03

RESERVED FINAL TEST

Use it only after freezing the candidate; document its limits.
