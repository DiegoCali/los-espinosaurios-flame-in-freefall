01 / WHY MICROGRAVITY

SAME FLAME. NO BUOYANCY.

Gravity changes how a flame receives oxygen. Switch the regime to see the conceptual difference described in the BASS-II knowledge base.

BUOYANT PLUME

ON EARTH, HOT GASES RISE

Buoyancy creates a rising plume that stretches the flame upward. Yellow-orange light is incandescent soot.

SHAPE
Tall teardrop
O₂ SUPPLY
Drawn in by the plume

COLOURBlue is chemiluminescence (CH*, C₂*). Yellow-orange is incandescent soot. At low flow less soot forms and the flame appears blue.
