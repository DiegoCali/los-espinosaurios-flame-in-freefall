The objectives of the SAFFIRE (Spacecraft Fire Experiment) experiments are to (1) determine how rapidly a large scale fire grows in low-gravity and (2) investigate the low-g flammability limits compared to those obtained in NASA’s normal gravity material flammability screening test. The specific objective of SAFFIRE-II tests is to quantify that affect in long-duration low-gravity using samples of the same size as those used in the NASA-STD-6001 Test 1 normal-gravity tests. In addition to the 9 samples, data was also collected for pre-flow and post-flow. These image sets are denoted as F1 for pre-flow and F2 for post-flow.
The experiment data is organized in PSI by sample number and camera number. There were 2 cameras capturing images during the experiment and they are denoted as C1 for camera 1 and C2 for camera 2. There are various documents and associated information located on the different tabs within the SAFFIRE Investigations records in PSI. A very useful document to review first is the “SAFFIRE Objectives, Development and Status.pdf” document located on the “Overview” tab in the Summary Document section. Some image sets are very large. They may contain over 10,000 images which makes it impractical to display as thumbnails in the Image Gallery which appears on the “Image Data and Information” tab of the Videos & Images record of the image set. In the case of very large image sets, all the images are provided in zip files labeled “Original Image Files” on the “Image Data and Information” tab. These files can be downloaded and viewed on the local machine.
