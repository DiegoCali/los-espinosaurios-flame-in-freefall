Conduct BASS-2 (Burning and Suppression of Solids-2) operations by adding GN2 to the WV to reduce the O2 partial pressure in order to get a stable blue flame for longer periods of time.

9.3	Review step 9.4 for hardware location and sequence order.
This step must be performed without delay or loss of science will occur.

[TABLA]
CAUTION
1.	The following step will initiate the Still Camera to take an image once every second. A maximum of 100 images may be taken before the camera stops. Releasing the SHUTTER will also cause the camera to stop taking images. /  / 2.	The SLICE Ignitor takes time to cool after Ignition; to avoid damage to the ignitor or front window release the IGNITOR lever only to the point that the ignitor is not glowing for 10 seconds before fully releasing. In order to hold the IGNITOR lever and push the SHUTTER button simultaneously, Step 9.4 will be performed with right hand in the right MSG Glove Ring Assembly and the left hand on the SHUTTER button.
[/TABLA]





Figure 15. - SPICE Experiment Assembly - Flame Ignition.


SPICE Exp. Assy	9.4	Locate ignitor lever (Figure 15).
IGNITOR lever → Pull forward and hold (ignitor glows)

BASS Control Box	pb SHUTTER → Press firmly and lock.

Hold IGNITOR lever at mid-point for 10 seconds for ignitor to cool, and then fully release.

9.5	Adjust RADIOMETER GAIN until RAD value on MSG Video Monitor 1 is at highest value possible but RAD ≤ 1.0

9.6	After burning for approximately one minute, attempt to extinguish flame per PI direction.

If extinguishing the flame by terminating air flow,
Set FAN FLOW to 0.00
Wait for flame to extinguish.


If extinguishing the flame using GN2,
sw GN2 ENA → ON
Increase GN2 flow by one turn.
Observe flame for destabilization or extinction (wait 5 seconds).
Repeat until flame extinguishes.

sw GN2 ENA → OFF

10.	MSG WV PURGE

[TABLA]
WARNING
The MSG WV must be purged for 3 minutes after every ten BASS burns because of the concern of combustion byproducts from the Freon in the cabin air.
[/TABLA]


SPICE Exp. Assy	10.1	sw FUEL → OFF (pull toward front of WV)

CMP	10.2	sw FUNCTIONS AIR CIRC → ON (Hold switch in place for 1 second and manually return to center.)
✓FUNCTIONS AIR CIRC ON LED  ON (green)

10.3	Remove front MSG Glove Ring Assembly (two) (MSG goes to OPEN mode)

10.4	Wait 3 minutes while Work Volume clears of combustion products before accessing the WV.

10.5	While waiting an additional 6 minutes with FAN FLOW at maximum for touch temperature,
Per PI direction, perform 2.007 BASS FLASH CARD EXCHANGE, steps 2 to 4 (US PODF: MSG BASS)

SPICE Exp. Assy	10.6	If required, adjust sample distance and/or BASS Ignitor per PI direction for next test point.

10.7	Install front MSG Glove Ring Assembly (two).

10.8	sw FUNCTIONS AIR CIRC → OFF (Hold switch in place for 1 second and manually return to center.)
✓FUNCTIONS AIR CIRC OFF LED  On (green, air circulation disabled)

10.9	If additional reduced O2 tests planned for the day,
Perform step 7 then exit procedure.


If additional test in normal air planned for the day,
Go to step 8.5


If all tests complete for the day,
Go to step 11
