Test Matrix explanation
Tabs in spreadsheet for each PI.
Subrata Bhattacharjee burned various thickness of very thin pmma films.
Paul Ferkul burned Nomex III and a candle to round out the BASS-I testing.
Fletcher Miller burned various thickness and widths of thick pmma slabs (one sided and two sided burns).
Sandra Olson burned three sizes of pmma rods (mostly clear), igniting the upstream end for a stagnation flame / concurrent flow geometry.
Carlos Fernandez-Pello burned three sizes of pmma rods (mostly black), igniting the downstream end for an opposed flow flame spread geometry.
James T’ien burned mostly SIBAL fabric (75/25 cotton/fiberglass blend).  Also had 1 sample/2 tests with 4 cm pmma spherical section.

Columns in test matrix:
Date:  actual date of experiment.  Video display is one day later than actual date due to a leap year the on-board computer did not account for.
GMT- number is day of the year.  ISS runs on Greenwich mean time, so their clock reads as GMT – Year – Day - time
Test number:   Initial of PI name followed by test number from the initial matrix.   If multiple runs with same sample on same day, a and b are used to designate.
Sample number:   some samples were used for multiple runs.
Material:  self explanatory
Flow restrictors:  perforated plates that could be attached to the inlet screen to increase the flow restriction and thus reduce the flow range.   0 is highest flow, lowest restriction; and 2 is lowest flow, highest restriction.  
Fan display:  instantaneous fan voltagex10 on the video display.  Maximum was 122, or 12.2 volts.  Also listed sometimes are pot settings on the control box.  10 is max.
Air display:  flow speed measured in the duct in cm/s on the video display.  Response time is very slow (~ 1 min), so reading is only steady for long times at the same flow setting.
Radiometer gain:   Uncalibrated radiometer gain, radiometer is displayed on the video.   Adjusted to avoid saturation.  Useful for relative flame intensity readings and flame dynamics (like pre-extinction oscillations).  Video display data rate is 5 Hz.
Total frames:  digital Nikon still image frames shot, includes test and post-burn sample images.  
Calibrated initial and final O2:   The O2 sensor was out of calibration, so the MCA ISS reading was used to correct the initial ISS ‘air’ reading of the sensor for the day via a constant offset.     
CO2 readings:  CDM sensor readings. 
CO readings:  CSA-CP sensor readings.  OR = over range (> 1000 ppm)


Videos:   most test videos have the test number (PI initial+ # from test matrix)  In the Misc video folder, there are backup test recordings, recordings of crew operations, etc.
